Privacy Policy for My Dogs

Last updated: September 13, 2026

My Dogs helps you organize information about your dog or dogs. Privacy is built into the app: the information you enter is stored on your device and, when you use iCloud, in Apple iCloud/CloudKit.

1. Contact

My Dogs is developed and provided by the app developer. For privacy questions, contact Hannevika@hotmail.com.

2. Information you enter

Depending on the features you use, the app may process information such as your dog's name, photos, date of birth, breed, sex, chip/ID details, health information, weight and measurements, vaccinations, medications, veterinary history, calendar events, diary entries, documents, food, routines, personality, expenses, walks, and other information you choose to enter.

The app does not require you to create a separate developer account.

3. Storage and iCloud

Data is stored locally on your device and may be synchronized through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services when iCloud is available and enabled. iCloud data is processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policies.

The developer does not operate a separate server that receives or stores the content you enter in the app.

4. Location and GPS walks

When you start recording a walk, My Dogs may request access to your location. Location is used to record and display the route on a map and to calculate walk information. Location is used in connection with walk recording and is stored as part of your walk data. An active walk may continue recording while the app is in the background.

You can deny or later change location access in iOS Settings. GPS-based walk recording will not work without location access.

5. Photos and documents

If you choose to add photos or documents, they are used only as part of the content you choose to store in the app. Access to your photo library is controlled by iOS and granted by you.

6. Notifications

My Dogs may request permission to send local notifications, for example for medications, calendar events, and care routines. You control whether notifications are allowed and can change this in iOS Settings.

7. Sharing

If you choose to share a dog with another person, the app uses Apple's CloudKit sharing features. People you invite may gain access to information included in the shared dog profile. Only share with people you trust. The owner can stop sharing.

8. Pro purchase

Pro features may be offered as a one-time purchase through the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple. The app uses StoreKit to determine purchase status. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

9. Tracking, advertising, and analytics

My Dogs does not use advertising networks and is not designed to track you across apps or websites. The app does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store and app analytics according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies.

10. Backup and export

If you use backup, export, or sharing features, you choose where the exported file is stored or who receives it. Once data is exported from the app, you are responsible for where that file is kept.

11. Retention and deletion

Information remains for as long as it exists on your device or in your iCloud account. You can delete content in the app. When a dog or shared profile is deleted, the app also attempts to remove related content and sharing from iCloud. Backups or files you previously exported must be deleted separately wherever you stored them.

12. Children

The app is not designed for the developer to collect personal information directly from children. If you enter information relating to other people, you are responsible for having the right to do so.

13. Health information

Health features are organizational tools for dog owners and do not constitute veterinary advice. The app does not replace examination, diagnosis, or treatment by a veterinarian.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or requirements change. The date above shows when it was last updated.
